Model boundaries

  • Point-mass translational 3-DOF dynamics
  • Flat-Earth Cartesian frame over short range
  • Constant air density in current build
  • Thrust, gravity, drag and variable mass included
  • Attitude dynamics and actuator lag excluded
  • Results valid only inside stated envelope
